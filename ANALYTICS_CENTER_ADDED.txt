Analytics Center added inside admin.html

How to use:
1. Deploy to Railway.
2. Login to admin.html as Super Admin.
3. Click the Analytics Center tab in the CMS buttons.
4. Visit the public website pages to generate tracking data:
   - index.html
   - shop.html
   - contact.html
   - account.html
   - track-order.html
5. Refresh Analytics Center.

What it tracks:
- Customer sessions
- Page views
- Clicks
- WhatsApp clicks
- View Details clicks
- Add to Cart clicks
- UTM source, medium, campaign, term, content
- Referrer
- Device type
- Top pages
- Top products
- Funnel events
- Abandoned carts endpoint

Campaign link example:
https://your-domain.com/shop.html?utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=riyadh_sofas

Important:
- Use the live Railway URL, not file:// local files, because tracking is saved through /api/journey.
- Super Admin can access analytics automatically.
- For regular admins, give them analytics read permission in Admin Users.
