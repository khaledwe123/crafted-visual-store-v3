HOW TO VIEW PRODUCT PROTOTYPE

1. Open admin.html.
2. Login as Super Admin.
3. Go to Products.
4. Add product information.
5. Add at least one color photo, then click Add Color Photos.
6. Click Save Product.
7. Click Open Shop Preview.

Important: Open Shop Preview now creates a guaranteed preview directly from the Super Admin products. It does not need a database and does not depend on shop.html/localStorage.

For real live publishing later, the site must be connected to a backend database/API.
